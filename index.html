Orc Quest

Moving and talking

  • Move: arrow keys or WASD
  • Talk, continue, confirm: Z, Space or Enter
  • Describe surroundings: E
  • Repeat last message: R

Battles and menus

  • Choose: up and down, or 1 to 6, then Z
  • Menu (status, items, equipment, save, load): M
  • Random battles happen in the forest
  • Brenna in the village sells gear and potions

Options

  • Captions on or off: C
  • Voice on or off: V, speed: [ and ]
  • Music and sound effects: N
