Orc Quest

Move: arrow keys or WASD. Z, Space or Enter: talk, continue, and confirm. E: describe surroundings. Battles: up and down to choose (or 1 to 5), Z to confirm. Random battles happen in the forest. M: menu (status, items, equipment, save, load). Brenna in the village sells gear and potions. R: repeat last message. H: high contrast. V: voice. [ and ]: voice speed. N: music and sound effects on or off.
